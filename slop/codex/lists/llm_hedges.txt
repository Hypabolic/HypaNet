# slop/codex/lists/llm_hedges.txt
# Hedges, softeners and vague attributions: words that make a claim unfalsifiable or borrow authority without naming
# a source. Patterns: L23 (capability hedges), L24 (vague attribution and authority), L25 (apologetic and
# reassuring softeners in system messages).
#
# Hedges in UI copy are double-edged. "May take up to 24 hours" is a real constraint (KEEP, LABELLING.md K4); "can
# help you" in a feature blurb says nothing. A rule should only report a hedge in promotional slots (hero, subtitle,
# feature card body, CTA band) or when it stacks with L01-L03 vocabulary, never in helper, error or legal text.
#
# Format: one phrase per line, lower case, natural spelling; fold before matching (rules/README.md). Lines starting
# with # are comments.
#
# Provenance: Wikipedia MOS:WEASEL and "Signs of AI writing" (vague attributions: "Industry reports", "Experts
# argue"; hedging disclaimers: "based on available information") are cited, CC BY-SA 4.0; only short stock phrases
# are reused. stop-slop phrases.md (MIT) names the softener adverbs. The UI-specific entries are HypaNet's own
# ("added"). Licence: MIT (the HypaNet repo licence); phrases attributed to Wikipedia are common English and are
# listed with attribution.
